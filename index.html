EmergencyReady

The Problem

Emergencies can occur any time and anywhere. In emergencies, people often feel frightened, stressed, and confused due to which they fail to think rationally. In these crucial moments, knowledge and the right decisions can make a big difference.

Our Solution

Through our company, we aim at preparing people for emergencies, teaching them how to act safely and efficiently during intense situations. Rather than just giving them the reading materials, we wish to create an interactive experience through which people can practice decision-making in life-like emergency situations.

Our users will get to know the most appropriate actions in different emergency conditions through our platform and learn why they should select one or the other decision. We are not going to replace the emergency specialists, though. We will make an attempt to equip ordinary people with the necessary knowledge and preparedness to keep calm and act correctly.
